## Building at Synthetiq

I care about software that is useful, considered, and respectful of the person using it. That means clear products, local-first choices where they make sense, and less noise between someone and the thing they want to do.

| Product | What it is | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Synthetiq Player](https://synthetiq.uk/products) | A personal media app designed around control, playback, and a calm experience. | Live on the App Store · around 5,000 active users |
| [Synthetiq Books](https://synthetiq.uk/products) | A focused iPhone and iPad reading library for PDF and EPUB publications you choose to import and connect. | Coming soon · projected 300–500 first-week users |
| [AROKI](https://synthetiq.uk/products) | A private, on-device media-library and native playback app for approved personal collections. | In development |

## Selected public work

- [Synthetiq Invoices](https://github.com/kas021/Synthetiq-Invoices) — a privacy-first, offline invoice generator.
- [Employee NFC Logging Time Sheet](https://github.com/kas021/Employee-NFC-Logging-Time-Sheet) — Raspberry Pi and NFC-based attendance logging for Google Sheets.
- [Hypocrisy Detector](https://github.com/kas021/Hypocrisy-Detector-) — a local-first research prototype for surfacing potentially contradictory public statements with linked evidence.
